# Comment.AI Pitch

## One-liner

Comment.AI turns online comments and reviews into a clean sentiment dashboard.

## The problem

People do not have time to read hundreds of reviews or comment threads. Businesses, students, creators, and researchers need a faster way to understand what people actually feel.

## The solution

Paste a Reddit, Amazon, or webpage URL into Comment.AI. The app scrapes the available comments/reviews, analyses sentiment and emotion, and shows an easy dashboard with charts and per-comment insights.

## Target users

- Student researchers
- Small businesses
- Product teams
- Content creators
- Market research beginners

## Tech used

Python, Streamlit, BeautifulSoup, Requests, Pandas, Plotly, VADER, and Transformers.

## What makes it useful

It combines scraping, NLP, and dashboarding into one simple prototype that can be demonstrated live during a hackathon.

## Next steps

Deploy it online, add export features, improve sarcasm detection, and support more websites reliably.
